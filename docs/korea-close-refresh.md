# Domestic close: narrow updates
- Domestic market close: update only `data/korea-close-latest.json` when verified figures change. The existing archive is not rewritten. The close entry and source IDs date each data group.
- `marketScreens.netBuys[market][investor]` accepts a same-session verified positive amount ranking with `asOf`, `unit`, `rows` of code/name/value, and entry source IDs. An estimated or zero-money list is not a ranking; the UI retains the most recent valid date independently per table.
- `marketScreens.turnoverMovers` requires `scope: krx-regular-close-turnover`, `asOf`, actual previous exchange session, source IDs, and dated rows with both sessions' closes and actual won turnover. The UI computes both inclusive thresholds; never substitute volume or price times volume.
- Do not merge provisional market flow into the published daily cash flow series or derive market credit splits from the association's total. Market-wide official totals and the three historical charts have independent dates.
- Domestic ETFs render one 12-month daily series. Gather only completed, same-basis, publishable dated daily candles and an overlapping archival boundary. After separately verifying the exchange calendar and public redistribution rights, run `python scripts/prepare_korean_etf_daily.py --input verified-ohlc.json --sessions YYYY-MM-DD,YYYY-MM-DD` first without `--apply`. `--apply` updates a local candidate only. The helper performs no fetches, does not change tags, groups, holdings, intraday or archived positive prices, and requires the verified dates since the retained boundary without gaps. Verify exact candidate checks before publishing; do not publish unchanged or partial funds as if the whole tab updated.
